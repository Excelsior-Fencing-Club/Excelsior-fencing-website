
## Fall registrations

You may have noticed that registrations did not auto-renew on September 1st. This was intentional.

Last year, due to changing life priorities and situations, many people did not return in the fall but got billed, which they found inconvenient.  
As such, we had disabled auto-renewal of memberships for September.

For returning members, please go to [https://www.wellnessliving.com/login/excelsior_fencing_club](https://www.wellnessliving.com/login/excelsior_fencing_club) and sign up. Auto-renewal is set for all the way to June.
