
## Head and body wires policy change

Going forward, the club will not be lending out head and body wires to members unless it is part of the paid rental.

Over the last year, the club has had to replace the various head and body wires at an unexpected and cumulatively expensive rate, due to damage or not being returned.

The wires can be purchased from  [https://imexsport.ca/](https://imexsport.ca/) or [https://www.leonpaulcanada.com/](https://www.leonpaulcanada.com/).
